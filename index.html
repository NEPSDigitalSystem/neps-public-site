Context About the NEPS Study

Clinical Scope & Target Population

NEPS (Navigating Educational Pressures and Stressors) is a clinical-grade digital mental health surveillance platform designed to evaluate and strengthen psychological resilience among adolescents and youth aged 12–24 years across West and East Africa over a 24-month longitudinal cohort study.

Under the Master CRF V3.1/V3.2 standard, the platform computes 9 authoritative psychometric indices to stratify risk and resilience:

K6 / MHDI (Mental Health Distress) BRS (Brief Resilience) MSPSS (Social Support) GSE (Self-Efficacy) EPI (Educational Pressure) ESI (Economic Stress) SBI (Stigma Burden) PFI (Protective Factors) NSES Composite Score

The platform combines REDCap survey capture with a high-throughput ETL scoring warehouse, a secure FastAPI backend, machine learning risk engines, and role-scoped Next.js clinical dashboards.

External Utility Sandboxed REDCap API Service

mock-redcap-service  Live on Render · V3.2 Standard

A high-fidelity standalone mock REDCap service hosted on Render that replicates exact REDCap API endpoints, authentication headers, and multi-country response payloads. Updated to the Version 3.2 data dictionary, it generates realistic synthetic participant profiles with automated DOB-to-age calculation, explicit consent gating, and stamps survey completion dates.

Exposes the full 150 participant baseline dataset across Ghana, Sierra Leone, and Tanzania, the 227-field data dictionary API, and the 2,000-record multimodal NLP dataset. All services point to this instance for end-to-end integration testing, providing a zero-friction drop-in replacement when the live KCHR REDCap production server (https://kchr.org) is connected.
