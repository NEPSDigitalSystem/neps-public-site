Context About the NEPS Study

What is NEPS Digital?

NEPS (Navigating Educational Pressures and Stressors) is a clinical-grade digital health surveillance platform monitoring the mental health and psychological resilience of youth aged 10–24 across three African countries over a 24-month longitudinal study.

The platform integrates REDCap for structured psychosocial data collection, an AI/ML distress engine for real-time risk scoring (PHQ-9, GAD-7, suicidality screening), and role-based clinical dashboards for Investigators, Country Leads, Enumerators, and System Administrators.

Infrastructure is hosted on-premises at KNUST/CAIH, Ghana, with VPN-secured access for field teams in Sierra Leone and Tanzania. All data remains on-premise; no patient data is transmitted to cloud services.

External Development Utilities

mock-redcap-service  Live on Render

A standalone hosted mock REDCap API that mirrors exact REDCap POST endpoint payload formats. Serves realistic longitudinal youth mental health data across all three study countries. All teams (backend, data-platform, portal) point to this service for development and integration testing while the live REDCap instance is being provisioned.

The service includes a 2,000-record mock NLP dataset validated against the NEPS instrument schema. It also exposes REDCap-style record export, metadata, and event-arm endpoints, making it a true drop-in replacement that can be swapped by changing a single REDCAP_API_URL environment variable.
